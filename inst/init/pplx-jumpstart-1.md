## Key Color Notes

**Verified against official brand guidelines:**

- Ubuntu's orange `#E95420` is confirmed in their official brand palette alongside white and black [^1_5]
- Debian's red `#A80030` matches the color used on debian.org [^1_6]
- Fedora's primary blue `#0B57A4` is pulled directly from the Fedora logo color scheme [^1_7]
- Arch Linux's cyan `#1793D1` is the value referenced from the official arch art page [^1_8]
- Manjaro's green `#34BE5B` comes from the Wikimedia Commons-registered brand color [^1_9]
- openSUSE's green `#73BA25` is a well-documented community reference [^1_10]
- Pop!\_OS yellow `#FAA41A` reflects their post-COSMIC rebranding signature accent [^1_10]

**A few worth calling out aesthetically:**

- **EndeavourOS** uses a very distinctive deep magenta-purple, a deliberate contrast to Arch's cooler blue
- **Garuda Linux** leans into a fiery red-orange, matching its dragon mascot and gaming focus [^1_3]
- **CachyOS** uses a teal/aqua that visually signals its performance-tuned, modern Arch-derivative identity [^1_1]
